Recommended start

Prove one conversion system before expanding.

First 30-day test

Booking, SMS, landing page, plus referrals.

Choose med spas or elective clinics as the first beachhead. Appointment value is visible, lead conversion is familiar, and no-shows are painful. Keep v1 out of regulated health-data flows: collect only contact and booking preferences, then route clinical detail to the client's existing system. Add a review-and-referral engine as a component of the conversion bundle.

Commercial model

Implementation funds the launch. Monthly fees fund the system.

A mandatory setup fee plus a three-month managed-service commitment prevents the company from becoming a one-off website shop.

Commercial rules: three-month minimum after go-live, then month-to-month with 30 days' notice. Collect 50% of setup at kickoff and 50% at go-live. Bill usage costs separately. Use written change orders for new workflows, pages, custom software, or locations. Offer founder-design-partner pricing only for baseline access, a testimonial if successful, and an anonymized case study.

Execution

Decide, validate, systematize, sell.

Choose

  1. Select one beachhead vertical for 30 days.
  2. Freeze the v1 bundle: landing page, booking, SMS, reporting.
  3. Choose five name finalists for conflict checks.

Validate

  1. Create a one-page offer and fictional demo.
  2. Interview 10 owners or operators.
  3. Seek two paid design partners, not free pilots.

Systematize

  1. Choose a reusable landing, form, calendar, SMS, CRM, and automation stack.
  2. Create onboarding, access, copy, report, consent, and offboarding templates.
  3. Track speed to lead, bookings, shows, reactivation, and attributable revenue.

Sell

  1. Build a 100-prospect list in one geography.
  2. Lead with missed calls, slow follow-up, or no-shows - not AI.
  3. Close, measure, and publish the first case study.

Parallel discipline

Protect the Riamatic workstream.

The parallel venture should create learning and cash, not become a distraction from the RIA plan.

Riamatic mastery

Continue approximately three Transition To RIA episodes per week, source reading, and the 30-minute founders' synthesis.

Output: August, September, and October tracker milestones.

Venture validation

Run two founder sessions and five buyer conversations each week until the first paid design partner.

Output: beachhead, v1 offer, name, and two paid partners.

Founder review

Hold a 30-minute weekly review of tracker progress, pipeline, cash spent, and competing capacity demands.

Output: deliberate trade-offs, not accidental drift.
Capacity rule

Do not begin a second parallel offer until the first reaches two paying clients or is explicitly stopped after validation. The Riamatic study cadence is the input that makes the RIA venture defensible.
