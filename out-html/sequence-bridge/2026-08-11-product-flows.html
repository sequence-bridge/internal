Layer 1 · patient journey

A managed inquiry becomes a PMS appointment

Tracking-number call

  1. Choose English or press 9 / say “Español.”
  2. Optionally consent to scheduling, reminder, and disconnected-call texts.
  3. Press 1 to book or 2 for the office; 0 is the later global human escape.
  4. Speech is bounded. Raw SpeechResult is transient; only normalized results persist.

Web or conversational SMS

  1. Capture a new-patient preference and consent state.
  2. Offer two or three live Synchronizer slots.
  3. Collect required identity fields through the secure form.
  4. Match before create, revalidate, write, then confirm.

Warm transfer

Twenty seconds by default, configurable from 10–30. Only provider outcome human bridges. Machine/fax, busy, no-answer, failed, canceled, unknown, timeout, and hang-up use the safe fallback.

Honest gaps

Direct calls to the practice main line are invisible. Existing patients go to the office/request-to-book. No SMS consent means no automated follow-up. No-show recovery is not in v1.

Layer 2 · what we install

One operated platform, two critical vendors

Inside Sequence Bridge

  • Landing page, secure form, tracking number, bounded IVR
  • Consent/suppression ledger and minimum event/audit store
  • Patient matching, creation idempotency, booking engine
  • Request-to-book workbench and secure task links
  • Confirmation, reminders, referral ask, and one follow-up
  • Operator dashboard and monthly report

Systems of record / vendors

  • Practice PMS via NexHealth Synchronizer
  • Twilio Voice, Messaging, Speech, and AMD
  • Per-practice A2P sender/campaign
  • Practice main line, unchanged
  • Email rail for opaque task notifications

Synchronizer circuit

Three transient failures in two minutes or one auth/permission failure opens it. Every channel switches to request-to-book with a 24-clock-hour response promise. No automatic replay.

Voice fallback

If the primary IVR handler fails, a provider-hosted handler forwards directly to the practice main line. Reachability survives even when automation is impaired.

Data boundary

Scheduling/messaging may be PHI. Clinical intake, raw audio/transcripts, unrestricted notes, charts, insurance, claims, and billing remain outside.

Resolved contract

What v1 means now

PMS

Synchronizer only. Target Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Curve Hero, and Dentrix Ascend, subject to exact operation/version validation. No hold slots.

Patient

Phone is candidate lookup only. DTMF DOB precedes first-name confirmation. New demographics use the secure form. Never auto-merge.

Phone

Bilingual bounded IVR, early optional consent, deterministic AMD fallback, one consented follow-up, and provider-hosted forwarding.

Speech

Transient Twilio recognition is in. Raw persistence, recording, clinical interpretation, and open-ended conversation are out.

Referral

Basic completed-visit ask and engagement metrics are in. Unique-code attribution and referred bookings/shows are v2.

Reporting

Only managed channels enter the funnel. Cost per booked appointment requires client-supplied spend.
