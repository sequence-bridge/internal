Option A — Recommended
RIA Sequential Roadmap
Commit to the RIA vertical and execute a phased build toward Q1 2027 cold outreach. Each phase gates the next.
~12 months Sequential RIA vertical locked
Phase 1
Foundation
Weeks 1–4
  • Form US LLC (Delaware or Wyoming — lowest friction for non-residents)
  • Set up Ecuador contractor structure — Hector + Cynthia invoice the LLC as independent contractors
  • Draft operating agreement: equity split, decision rights, vesting/cliff, roles
  • Open US business bank account (Mercury is remote-founder friendly)
  • Register for EIN; confirm US tax obligations as foreign-owned LLC
  • Decide on division of labor: Hector builds + PMs, Cynthia sells + manages client relationships
Gate: Legal entity in place and partnership terms signed before spending on tools or vendors.
Phase 2
Domain Learning & Validation
Weeks 5–16
  • Deep-dive study: RIA workflows, Rule 206(4)-7, Marketing Rule, Form ADV, Reg S-P (June 3 deadline is a content hook even pre-launch)
  • Competitive scan: what tools small RIAs already use (Orion, Redtail, Wealthbox, Schwab Advisor Center)
  • Engage a paid domain advisor — an active RIA principal or compliance consultant for 4–6 hours/month
  • Run 10 customer discovery calls with RIA founders ($100M–$500M AUM); goal: validate pain points and willingness to pay
  • Document the "moment of pain" in onboarding: where does time actually go?
Gate: At least 1 RIA says "I would pay $5k/month for that today." If zero, revisit scope before building.
Phase 3
Spec & Architecture
Weeks 14–20
  • Spec the Onboarding Accelerator web app: screen flows, user roles (advisor, CCO, client), data model
  • Spec the AI Governance Kit: policy templates, vendor diligence checklists, Form ADV update guide, audit log structure
  • Define the Claude integration layer: which agents, what prompts, what guardrails
  • Shortlist 2–3 AML vendors for the paid add-on (ComplyAdvantage, LexisNexis Bridger)
  • Choose tech stack and set up dev environment
Overlaps with Phase 2 — start spec conversations with early discovery call participants so they shape the product.
Phase 4
Build Offering #1
Weeks 18–36
  • Build the agency web app: status dashboard, CCO audit log view, advisor task queue, client-facing status page
  • Integrate Claude as the document validation + KYC completeness agent
  • Build Zapier/n8n workflow engine: intake form → document collection → validation → custodial pre-fill → e-signature routing
  • Assemble AI Governance Kit deliverables (policy templates, Form ADV guidance, validation test plan)
  • Wire AML vendor integration for the paid add-on
  • Internal pilot: run 2–3 onboardings manually + with the tool to validate time savings
Gate: End-to-end onboarding flow works in a staging environment; at least one discovery call participant has seen a demo and given feedback.
↔ Runs in parallel with Phases 3–4
Phase 5
Content Engine
Weeks 14–36+
  • First piece (publish ASAP): Reg S-P + AI compliance checklist for RIAs — ties to June 3, 2026 deadline; SEO + authority signal
  • Content pillars: RIA compliance, AI in advisory workflows, productivity for founder-PMs
  • Formats: long-form blog + short-form video (LinkedIn/YouTube)
  • Build SEC-IAPD ICP list: Tier 1 (small SEC-RIAs $100M–$500M) + Tier 2 (FL/TX state-registered $50M–$80M)
  • Identify 2–3 RIA podcasts for guest appearances (builds credibility before outbound)
  • Both founders post on LinkedIn — build personal brand alongside agency brand
Phase 6
Go to Market
Q1 2027 →
  • Cold outbound begins: LinkedIn + email sequences to Tier 1 ICP list
  • Lead with the AI Governance Kit as a free diagnostic (5-question email audit) — lowers barrier, surfaces anxiety
  • Offer a paid pilot at a reduced rate ($2.5k/mo for 90 days) to get first 3 reference customers
  • Podcast guesting: target RIA-adjacent shows (Kitces, Financial Advisor Success, etc.)
  • Reference customer case studies ready by end of Q1 2027
Target: 3 paying reference customers by end of Q2 2027. Then price to full $5k–$7k/mo.
Option B — If Vertical Isn't Locked
Structured Vertical Selection
Evaluate 3–5 candidate verticals before committing. Run in parallel with Option A's Phase 1 if Cynthia isn't aligned on RIA yet.
~10 weeks Data-driven Feeds into Option A
Step 1
Define Selection Criteria
Week 1
  • Must-haves: SMB size, back-office workflows, founder is the buyer, regulatory complexity creates moat, proven willingness to pay ~$5k/mo
  • Nice-to-haves: workflows repeat across clients (not bespoke), underserved by enterprise platforms, at least one founder has domain passion
  • Score each criterion 1–5 for each vertical; weight the must-haves 2×

Step 3
Desk Research Score
Weeks 2–4
  • For each of top 3 verticals: research market size, price benchmarks, competitive landscape, and regulatory burden
  • Score each 1–5 on: TAM, willingness to pay, workflow repeatability, competitive gap, regulatory moat
  • Identify whether a $5k/mo subscription has precedent in each vertical
  • Document sources; keep it lean — 1–2 pages per vertical is enough
Step 4
Customer Discovery
Weeks 4–10
  • Run 5 discovery calls per top 2 verticals (10 total)
  • Script: "Tell me how a new client gets onboarded at your firm. Walk me through it step by step." — let them reveal the pain
  • End every call with: "If a tool cut that in half, what would you pay monthly for it?"
  • Track: pain severity, current tools, budget signals, who else to talk to
  • Cynthia leads the calls; Hector takes notes and looks for patterns
Gate: At least one vertical produces 3+ people who say a number ≥ $3k/mo unprompted.
⊕
Joint Decision Meeting
Week 10–11 · Hector + Cynthia review scores, call notes, and gut read together. Pick ONE vertical. This meeting ends with a written decision.
Step 5
Align on the Plan
Week 11
  • Document the decision and rationale in writing — revisit only if a hard blocker emerges
  • If RIA wins (expected): pull the RIA Offerings draft back out and treat it as v1 of the product spec
  • If another vertical wins: run a targeted version of the RIA offerings doc for that segment
After Step 5, this path merges into Option A, Phase 2 (Domain Learning & Validation)
Rec.
Run both in parallel — Option B first, then Option A from Phase 2
Start Option A's Phase 1 (entity formation) immediately — that work is vertical-agnostic and there's no reason to wait. Run Option B (vertical selection) concurrently over the first 10 weeks. If RIA wins the decision meeting (likely), skip Option A's Phase 2 validation and go straight to Domain Learning — the discovery calls in Option B already cover it. This approach gives Cynthia a structured process to get to conviction, without delaying the legal and partnership work that must happen regardless.
