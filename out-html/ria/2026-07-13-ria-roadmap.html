Phase 3 · Moved here, after Spec
Entity & Legal Foundation
Oct – Nov 2026
  • Engage a Florida business attorney + a US/Ecuador cross-border tax advisor — validate the vehicle first: is a Florida LLC right for an entity owned entirely by two non-US-resident members, or is a C-corp / alternative better?
  • Resolve the Term Sheet's §19 open items (revenue-phase trigger, fundamental-matters carve-out + debt threshold, buyout fair-value method, non-compete scope).
  • Form the entity; obtain EIN + ITINs for both founders.
  • Open a US business bank account (Mercury is remote/foreign-founder friendly).
  • Convert the signed Term Sheet into a formal Operating Agreement (supersedes the Term Sheet).
Why after Spec: the signed Term Sheet already governs the founders' relationship, and forming an all-foreign-owned US entity + cross-border tax setup is a real cash and complexity outlay. Defer it until validation (Phase 1 gate) and spec confirm the build is real — but it must be finished before any client revenue lands.
Gate: Entity, EIN, and banking live before the Q1 2027 pilot invoices anyone. Build (Phase 4) does not depend on this and can run in parallel.
